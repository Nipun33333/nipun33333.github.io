Personal YouTube Content Automation

Video research, creation
and publishing — automated.

YTADGENT AI is a personal Python-based workflow that researches trending topics in the United States, prepares scripts and video assets, renders videos, and publishes videos to a YouTube channel selected and authorized by its owner.

View source projectRead our privacy policy

Topic research

Discovers current US trending subjects and prepares content ideas.

Video production

Creates scripts, narration, visual assets and rendered video using configured services.

Owner-approved access

Uploads through the YouTube Data API using OAuth authorization from the channel owner.

How YouTube authorization works

The owner authenticates with Google's official consent page. The automation requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) to publish videos to the authorized channel. OAuth credentials are not collected by this website, and this website does not provide a public login or upload service.

Project and support

YTADGENT AI is intended for its owner's personal content workflow, not as a multi-user hosted SaaS. For source code and project details, see the GitHub repository. For questions, contact skelaton67trending@gmail.com.
