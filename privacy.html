Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YTADGENT AI, a personal YouTube content automation project maintained by its owner, uses Google user data when authorized to publish videos. The public website is informational and does not have account registration or an OAuth sign-in form.

1. Google data and permission accessed

YTADGENT AI requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload through Google's OAuth 2.0 consent process. This authorization permits the tool to upload videos and associated titles, descriptions, tags, and visibility settings to the YouTube channel the owner authorizes. The app is not designed to download YouTube private messages, contacts, or unrelated Google account data.

2. Purpose of access

Access is used only to publish video content selected or generated by the owner-configured automation, and to complete related upload actions (for example, setting a video thumbnail where supported). The automation also uses configured services for topic research and content generation. YouTube OAuth tokens are not sent to those content-generation providers.

3. Token storage and safeguards

The owner configures OAuth credentials in encrypted GitHub Actions repository secrets for automated workflows. Locally generated credentials may be stored in a local credentials file on the owner's computer. During scheduled runs, access tokens may exist temporarily in the execution environment. The website itself never requests, receives, or stores YouTube OAuth credentials. Users must not submit secrets in public issues or source code.

4. Data sharing

The automation sends authorized upload data and OAuth authorization to Google's YouTube API for the purpose of video publishing. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising profiles, or share YouTube OAuth tokens with data brokers. GitHub hosts the static website and workflow infrastructure, subject to GitHub's own privacy practices. Video titles, descriptions and media published to YouTube are visible according to the selected video's privacy settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Uploaded videos are retained on YouTube until the channel owner modifies or deletes them. Workflow outputs and locally stored OAuth credentials remain under the owner's control; generated temporary runner files are not intended to be long-term storage. To revoke this tool's authorization, the Google Account holder can remove its access at Google Account connections, and delete the stored token or GitHub Actions secrets. The tool will then be unable to upload until reauthorized.

6. Security and third parties

The project uses OAuth rather than collecting a Google password. It uses Google's YouTube API Services and is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. This public website does not intentionally set marketing tracking cookies or run advertising scripts. GitHub may process standard hosting logs.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data handling change. Changes will be published on this page. Questions about this project or its data practices may be sent to skelaton67trending@gmail.com.
